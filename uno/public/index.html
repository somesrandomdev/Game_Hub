    How to play

    📶 Connecting (no internet needed)

    1. One PC runs start.bat (that's the server).
    2. Everyone must be on the same network: the same Wi-Fi router, or turn on Windows Mobile Hotspot (Settings → Network → Mobile hotspot) and have friends join it. The router doesn't need internet.
    3. Friends open the address shown (like http://192.168.1.8:3000) in any browser, including phones.
    4. Can't connect? Run allow-firewall.bat once on the server PC, or click "Allow" on the Windows Firewall popup.

    🃏 Basics

    Match the top card by color or number/symbol, or play a Wild. Can't (or don't want to)? Draw a card. If it's playable you may play it right away. First to empty their hand wins the round and scores points for every card left in the other players' hands (numbers = face value, action cards = 20, wilds = 50).

    ⚡ Action cards

    • Skip: the next player loses their turn.
    • Reverse: direction flips (with 2 players it acts like Skip).
    • Draw Two: the next player draws 2 and loses their turn.
    • Wild: choose the next color.
    • Wild Draw Four: choose the color, the next player draws 4 and loses their turn. It's only legal if you have no card of the current color. The victim may challenge: if you bluffed, you draw 4; if you were honest, they draw 6.

    📣 UNO!

    Hit UNO! when you're down to 2 cards (before playing) or right after you drop to 1. If you forget, anyone can hit Catch! on you before the next player acts, and you draw a penalty.

    🏠 House rules (host picks in the lobby)

    • Stacking: answer a +2 with a +2 or +4, or a +4 with a +4. The penalty grows until someone can't stack.
    • Draw until playable: keep drawing until you get a card you can play.
    • Forced play: if the drawn card is playable, you must play it.
    • 7-0: a 7 swaps your hand with a player of your choice; a 0 passes every hand along in the direction of play.
    • Jump-in: holding the exact same card (color and number) as the top card? Play it any time, even out of turn. Play continues from you.
    • Turn timer: when time runs out, autopilot moves for you.

    💡 Tips

    • If someone disconnects, autopilot plays for them after 10s. Reopen the page to come back.
    • Add bots to fill the table or to practice solo.
    • Tap Sort to switch between sorting by color, by number, or not at all.
